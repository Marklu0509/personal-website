About

Hi, I'm Mark — a software engineer based in Sydney, by way of Taipei. Before software I trained and worked as an optometrist, which taught me something most codebases forget: the person using the tool is busy, tired, and doesn't care how clever the internals are.

Throughout my career change I've seen how quickly projects gain weight — extra process, extra handoffs, layers nobody questions. I learned to stop that from happening. I work across the full stack and the cloud, and I'm happiest turning a messy workflow into something clear and dependable.

Before landing in this sector, I spent years in clinical practice and APAC practice development, working with audiences across markets. I hold an MS in Computer Science, and that is where much of this all began.

What makes the difference

If you're a team or an independent organisation looking for help with your product or with the strategy, systems and integrations that surround it, I'll bend over backwards to lighten your workload and put your project on the path to success.

What sets me apart is my hands-on approach: I personally handle virtually every aspect of every feature, from data model to interface to deployment. This means fewer variables, lower overheads for you, and a more cohesive end product. I also keep maintainability and testing top of mind, both in the final result and in how I achieve it.

Sustainability

If there is one constant in my journey, it has been the desire to work for worthy causes — and the only way to fulfil that desire is to rethink what matters most. Software has a real footprint: every dependency, every always-on service, every megabyte shipped to a phone costs energy somewhere.

So I build light by default. Static where static works, small bundles, boring infrastructure that doesn't run hot. This site itself is plain HTML and CSS — no framework, no tracking, nothing loading in the background.

Although my practice is far from perfect, I see every project as an opportunity to shape the change I wish to see. For the record, I prefer not to work with clients in sectors whose main line of business conflicts with that.
