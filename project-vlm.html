VLM Fine-Tuning & LLM-as-Judge Pipeline

A research pipeline that fine-tunes a vision-language model to describe images faithfully, paired with an automated "LLM-as-judge" system that measures how trustworthy those descriptions really are.

Project Info

Date2025
TeamUniversity research collaboration — 8 people
StackPython, PyTorch, LLaMA-Factory, vLLM, LoRA, Qwen3-VL, ArtcomBench
View on GitHub Go back

LoRA fine-tuning produced a model that beat all three baselines — including a 2025 state-of-the-art model — on every faithfulness metric, trained on a single GPU in under three hours.

To make "how trustworthy is this output" measurable and reproducible, I helped build ArtcomBench, a custom LLM-as-judge benchmark, and validated 3,500 AI-generated labels against human raters (ICC 0.82) before training. The result is quality you can quantify, not just claim.
