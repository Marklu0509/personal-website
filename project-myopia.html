Myopia Progression Prediction

A clinical decision-support tool that models how a child's short-sightedness may progress from only their first few months of data, giving clinicians an explainable early signal.

Project Info

Date2025
StackPython, scikit-learn, XGBoost, SHAP, Streamlit, pandas
Visit the website Go back

I benchmarked six models under leave-one-out cross-validation with leakage-safe pipelines, and engineered clinically-grounded features that a purely technical modeler would miss. Patient-data de-identification is built into the pipeline from the start.

Crucially, I used SHAP to confirm the model's logic matched established optometry theory — earning clinician trust rather than asking for it — and shipped it as a live decision-support tool that turns model outputs into an actionable early signal.
