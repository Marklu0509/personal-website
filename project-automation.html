Features

  • Always-on ingestion: processes a live event feed continuously, 24/7, without dropping or duplicating anything.
  • Built-in guardrails: price limits, per-event caps, and spend limits keep every position in check.
  • Full transparency: every forecast, entry, and realized result logged in one place.

Why I built it

I wanted to build and actually operate a genuinely reliable, always-on service, and learn production monitoring and incident response (SRE) hands-on rather than in theory.

Core technical

  • Raised request success rate from 71% to 99.7% by reverse-engineering a hidden upstream API rule from logs.
  • Cut incident detection from hours to under 90s with Prometheus/Grafana alerts that fire even on a crash.
  • Cut processing overhead 87% by batching fragmented events into single requests.
  • Guaranteed zero event loss or double-counting across restarts with database-level idempotency.
  • Made the service recover from hangs on its own, with a health check that fails when the poll loop stalls.
